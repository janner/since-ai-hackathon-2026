A small usability test: the Kaista demo

This is not an exam. We want to see how the tool works without guidance. Do the tasks yourself. Do not read the documentation. I will not help. If you cannot find something, write that down. The demo opens in a new tab; come back to this form after each task.

Open the demo in a new tab

Background

How do you do this work today? Who fetches the IFIC, how long does one package take to go through, and how many packages a week?

Task 1: automatic fetch

Look at the top bar. Which IFIC package was fetched last, and how many notices did it hold?

Task 2: the case

Pick the top case in the queue. In your own words: why is it on the list, and what information is it missing?

Task 3: next step

What would you do about this case next? Where on the card do you see that?

Task 4: the undecidables

Find two notices the tool cannot decide. Where do they show up? If you cannot find them, write that.

Task 5: the calculation and your trust

Open "Show calculation" on the card. Do the PFD, the limit and the margin look right to you? Where would you check them?

How much would you trust this number?
